# What To Expect

## 1. What You Provide

One package of files covering your recruiting data (ATS/HRIS exports) and/or your workforce billing data (invoices, rate cards, contracts) — see `Client_Intake_Instructions.md` for the plain-language list of what to send.

## 2. What Happens After Intake

We check exactly what was received against what's needed for each part of the analysis. You'll know upfront if something's missing rather than being surprised at the end.

## 3. What Gets Analyzed

Two things, from the same intake:
- **Recruiting performance** — pipeline health, hiring efficiency, and (where data allows) retention and hire quality
- **Revenue/commercial exposure** — billing accuracy, rate compliance, contract adherence, and classification risk indicators

## 4. What You Receive

One report, delivered as both a Word document and a PDF. It opens with the headline findings, shows exactly what was and wasn't analyzed, and ends with specific recommended actions.

## 5. How Missing Data Affects the Report

If part of your data wasn't available, that section is clearly marked as unavailable in the report — nothing is estimated or filled in to look complete. Everything else that *does* have sufficient data is still fully analyzed and reported.

## 6. What the Report Helps You Do

Prioritize. Instead of a pile of raw metrics, you get a ranked view of what actually needs attention first — whether that's a hiring bottleneck, a compliance risk worth flagging to legal, or a specific dollar amount of billing that doesn't match your contracts.
