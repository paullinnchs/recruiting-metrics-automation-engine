# Service Overview

Turning recruiting, workforce, and commercial operating data into actionable intelligence — one intake, one report.

Most operations run recruiting performance and workforce billing as two completely separate conversations, tracked in different spreadsheets, reviewed by different people, on different schedules. This service brings both together into a single view, so you can see where hiring is underperforming and where money is quietly leaking out of your contingent workforce spend — at the same time, in the same report.

## Two Analysis Areas, One Report

### Recruiting Performance

We look at how your hiring process is actually functioning — things like how long roles stay open, how often offers get accepted, whether requisitions are getting filled at the rate you'd expect, and (where the data supports it) how new hires are performing and how long they stay. Every metric is compared against a threshold, so you see not just the number, but whether it's actually a problem worth your attention.

### Workforce Revenue Leak Workflow Sprint

Separately, we look at your contingent workforce billing — timesheets, rate cards, and contracts — and check for the ways revenue commonly leaks out of that spend: rates billed below what was agreed, billing continuing past an expired rate card, spend going through suppliers outside your approved channels, engagement patterns that may warrant a closer classification review, and hours that were approved but never invoiced (or invoiced beyond what was approved). Where the data supports it, each finding is quantified in dollars.

## One Consolidated Report

Both analyses come together into a single executive report — not two separate documents you have to reconcile yourself. It leads with the highest-priority findings across both areas, shows you exactly what data was and wasn't available, and closes with specific recommended next steps.

## What This Is — and Isn't

This is a structured diagnostic, run against the data you provide at a point in time. It is not a live dashboard and does not continuously monitor your systems — each report reflects a single analysis run against a single intake package. Any finding involving worker classification is presented as an operational risk indicator worth your legal or compliance team's review, not a legal conclusion.
