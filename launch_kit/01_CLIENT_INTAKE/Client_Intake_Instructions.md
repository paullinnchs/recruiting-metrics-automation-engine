# Workflow Sprint — What We Need From You

*Paul, what exactly do we need to send you?*

Short answer: **one package of files.** That single package lets us run both parts of the analysis — recruiting performance and revenue/commercial exposure — and turn them into one report.

You don't need to understand how the engine works internally. You just need to gather the files below and send them over.

---

## Two Kinds of Data, One Package

### 1. Recruiting Data

This is the information that shows how your hiring process is actually performing — how long roles stay open, how offers are going, and what happens to people after they're hired.

In plain terms, we need exports covering:

- **Open and closed requisitions/jobs** — what roles you've had open, when they were approved, and when (or if) they closed
- **Candidates and applications** — who applied, what happened to them, and where they came from
- **Offers** — who was offered a role and whether they accepted
- Optionally, **application activity** (started vs. completed applications) — this only affects one specific metric, so it's fine to skip if it's not easy to pull

For a deeper analysis that also looks at retention and hire quality, we additionally need:

- **Employee records** from your HR system — hire dates, terminations, departments
- **Performance ratings** for recent hires, if you track them
- **Candidate/employee survey responses**, if you run any (candidate experience, new-hire pulse, etc.)

If any of the above is hard to pull, that's okay — send what you have. We'll tell you exactly which parts of the analysis that affects.

### 2. Revenue / Commercial Data

This is the financial side — what you're actually billing and paying for contingent workforce, and whether it matches what was agreed.

We need:

- **Billing / invoice history** — what's been billed over the period in question, including hours and rates
- **Rate cards** — what you agreed to pay/bill per role or supplier
- **Active contracts or Statements of Work (SOWs)** — the terms each engagement is supposed to follow

All three of these are needed for the revenue side of the analysis to run.

---

## What Format?

CSV or JSON exports are both fine. If your systems only export Excel, that's usually workable too — just let us know before sending.

## What If We're Missing Something?

Send what you have. The report will clearly show which parts of the analysis ran on complete data and which parts couldn't be calculated because something was missing — nothing is estimated or guessed in place of a file we didn't receive.

## What You Get Back

One report — a single document that shows recruiting performance, revenue/commercial risk, and the specific things worth acting on first.
